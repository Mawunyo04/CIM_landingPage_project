Our Leaders

The men and women God has placed at the helm of Campus Invasion Movement.

Reverend

Dotun Arifalo

Dotun Arifalo is a prolific speaker, author, life and business and lifestyle mentor and empowerer, whose passion is contagious, initiating transformation in everyone that comes in contact with her.

She is the President of the Dotun Arifalo Ministries and the Co-Lead Pastor of Dominion House churches with a vision to raise world class Leaders.

RD, as fondly called, is an empowerer, a destiny catalyst and broker of gifts, an international conference speaker with an unusual grace of connecting with people of different races. She has a great passion to see men and women utilized, raised from mediocrity to maximizing their lives and God given potentials as they discover and fulfill God’s mandates for their lives.

She is married to Vincent Arifalo, her vision partner, and they have three children.

Pastor

Omoyeni Omolade

Pastor Yeni is the Lead Pastor and Head of Direction of Campus Invasion Movement North America, whose passion is to see young people grow spiritually from disciples to disciplers and take over their territories for Jesus.

PY, as fondly called, is a destiny catalyst, a movement maker and a person who is very passionate about filling campuses with the knowledge of the glory of the Lord as the waters cover the sea.
