About us

Who we are, and why we exist.

We exist to help students follow Jesus in public, ordinary ways — and to train them to invite others into the same life. CIM is not a performance. It is a community that prays, studies, serves, and multiplies.

  • Gather

    A weekly home for worship, Scripture, and honest conversation after class.

  • Grow

    Small groups and mentoring so faith is practiced, not just discussed.

  • Go

    Simple mission on campus and in the city — hospitality, service, and witness.

Join us

We are pleased to have you at the table.

Membership is open to students who want to belong, learn, and practice discipleship with others. No performance required — just a willing yes.

  • Come to a Thursday gathering first if you prefer.
  • Tell us your campus, year, and how to reach you.
  • We will follow up with next steps within a few days.
