Who we are

A Christian campus fellowship invading campuses with the gospel.

To raise kingdom leaders, for God’s end time harvest by the teaching of God’s Word, the move of His Spirit, and a viral discipleship movement on the campuses!

How we fulfill the mission

Three pillars that drive everything we do

The teaching of God’s Word

We ground students in Scripture so they grow as faithful, Spirit-led leaders for the harvest.

The move of His Spirit

We pursue the presence and power of the Holy Spirit in worship, prayer, and everyday campus life.

A viral discipleship movement

We multiply disciples on campus — students who make disciples until the gospel spreads across every hall and heart.
